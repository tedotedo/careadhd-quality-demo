From routine data to better care

Every referral form, rating scale, blood pressure reading and piece of feedback Care ADHD already collects can tell us how to make care faster, safer and fairer. This demo shows what that could look like, for adults and for children and young people with ADHD, autism, or both.

02 · Combined neurodevelopmental pathway

Autism and ADHD together, for children aged 7 and over

Autism and ADHD often occur together, and each changes how the other presents and responds to treatment. A combined pathway with shared data lets us see those interactions clearly, and plan care around the whole child rather than one diagnosis at a time.

What the combined data could tell us

  • Which children need slower titration, lower starting doses or earlier switches, so we plan fewer, better-informed steps.
  • Whether treating sleep first (behavioural plan, then melatonin where appropriate) improves stimulant tolerability.
  • How long the autism assessment takes against the CG128 3-month marker, and what speeds it up.
  • Whether outcomes are equal across deprivation, ethnicity and sex, including the under-recognition of autism in girls.
  • Which school information is most predictive, so we ask families for less but better information.

04 · Titration safety

Monitoring that never forgets

Blood pressure, heart rate, weight and height are recorded at every titration step. Simple rules, set by clinicians and aligned with NICE NG87 monitoring advice, can flag readings that need attention and observations that are overdue, so nothing waits for the next appointment.

09 · Governance

Safe, lawful and trusted by design

None of this is worth doing unless patients, families, clinicians and commissioners can trust it. Governance comes first, not last.

§UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018

Lawful basis and special category condition agreed with the Data Protection Officer. Clear privacy information for patients and families. Respect the National Data Opt-out where it applies.

8Caldicott principles

Justify every use, use identifiable data only when essential, the minimum necessary, need-to-know access, and the duty to share well for care. Caldicott Guardian sign-off.

DDPIA before anything is built

A Data Protection Impact Assessment for each new use, reviewed when the use changes. DSPT compliance maintained.

PMinimise and pseudonymise

Analytics run on pseudonymised extracts. Small numbers suppressed in reports. Engineering and testing done on synthetic data like this demo, so real data never needs to leave its secure home.

CSClinical safety: DCB0129 / DCB0160

Clinical Safety Officer, hazard log and safety case for any tool that touches care. MHRA guidance on software and AI as a medical device checked before any model influences individual decisions.

HHuman in the loop

Models suggest, clinicians decide. Every alert and AI extraction is reviewable, explainable and auditable, with regular checks for bias and drift.

Service evaluation, audit or research?

Most of this is service evaluation and audit, which can be done under existing governance. Anything intended to generate generalisable knowledge is checked with the HRA decision tool and given ethics approval where needed. Cross-border work with the Bengaluru team uses an agreed international transfer mechanism and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, with synthetic or pseudonymised data by default.

10 · UK + Bengaluru

Care ADHD's Global Capability Centre in Bengaluru already brings together engineers, data scientists and clinicians. Paired with UK clinical leadership, it can run a near round-the-clock learning cycle, and turn the results into published evidence that raises Care ADHD's profile in both countries.

  • Service evaluations and posters at UK meetings (for example UKAAN, RCPCH, RCPsych and BACCH conferences).
  • Papers in journals such as BMJ Open Quality and Archives of Disease in Childhood.
  • Quality reports for ICBs and commissioners: outcomes, safety and equity, not just activity.
  • Thought leadership on combined autism and ADHD care for children.

Building the profile in India

  • Showcasing Bengaluru as a hub for responsible health AI and data science, not only back-office support.
