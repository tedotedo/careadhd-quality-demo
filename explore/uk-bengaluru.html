UK + Bengaluru

One quality team across two time zones, and how the work could raise Care ADHD's profile in both countries.

Bengaluru early morning

Overnight data quality checks, refreshed dashboards and safety worklists ready before UK clinics open. Synthetic data pipelines for safe development.

Shared midday huddle

UK clinicians and Bengaluru analysts review alerts, model performance and improvement projects together. Clinical questions shape the analytics.

UK afternoon

Clinical leads test changes, Plan-Do-Study-Act cycles run in clinics, and findings go back to Bengaluru for the next iteration.

Promoting Care ADHD in the UK

  • Service evaluations and posters at UK meetings (for example UKAAN, RCPCH, RCPsych and BACCH conferences).
  • Papers in journals such as BMJ Open Quality and Archives of Disease in Childhood.
  • Quality reports for ICBs and commissioners: outcomes, safety and equity, not just activity.
  • Thought leadership on combined autism and ADHD care for children.

Building the profile in India

  • Showcasing Bengaluru as a hub for responsible health AI and data science, not only back-office support.
  • Joint UK–India talks and papers on synthetic data, explainable models and digital outcome measurement.
  • A data and quality framework ready for the planned Bengaluru clinic from day one.
  • Links with Indian academic partners and the UK–India health technology community.
