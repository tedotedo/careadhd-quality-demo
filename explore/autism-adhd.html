A combined neurodevelopmental pathway for children aged 7 and over, aligned with NICE CG128, CG170 and NG87.

One referral, one history, one joined-up formulation

Aligned with NICE CG128 (autism in under 19s: recognition, referral and diagnosis), NICE CG170 (autism in under 19s: support and management) and NICE NG87 (ADHD). Quality marker built in: autism diagnostic assessment started within 3 months of referral (CG128).

What the combined data could tell us

  • Which children need slower titration, lower starting doses or earlier switches, so we plan fewer, better-informed steps.
  • Whether treating sleep first (behavioural plan, then melatonin where appropriate) improves stimulant tolerability.
  • How long the autism assessment takes against the CG128 3-month marker, and what speeds it up.
  • Whether outcomes are equal across deprivation, ethnicity and sex, including the under-recognition of autism in girls.
  • Which school information is most predictive, so we ask families for less but better information.
