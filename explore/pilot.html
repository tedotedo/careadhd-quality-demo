90-day pilot and about me

A suggested 90-day pilot, what success would look like, and my background.

MA

Dr Mark Aszkenasy

Remote consultant, Care ADHD · Retired consultant community paediatrician

  • 30 years as a consultant community paediatrician, assessing and treating children with neurodevelopmental conditions.
  • Clinical lead for autism for 10 years, leading multidisciplinary autism diagnostic services.
  • Thousands of autistic children with sleep difficulties seen and supported: exactly the group where ADHD medication, sleep and anxiety interact.
  • Author of The Genetics of Autism.
  • GMC specialist registration in Public Health Medicine: population data, epidemiology, health inequalities and quality measurement.
  • Now working with Care ADHD as a remote consultant, so I understand the service from the inside.

"I can bridge the clinic and the dataset: asking the right clinical questions, making sure the answers are safe and meaningful, and turning them into evidence that helps children and adults."

Suggested 90-day pilot

Days 1–30

Discover and govern

  • Map data already collected across adult and Under 18s pathways.
  • Agree 6 to 8 quality measures with clinical leads, including combined autism + ADHD.
  • Choose the first NICE audit standards (e.g. NG87 monitoring, CG128 3-month start, NG43 transition).
  • DPIA, Caldicott and clinical safety sign-off.
  • Baseline report on pseudonymised data.
Days 31–60

Build with Bengaluru

  • Pathway, outcomes and safety dashboards.
  • SPC charts for 2 improvement projects (e.g. forms return, monitoring).
  • Prototype explainable engagement model with fairness audit.
  • Nightly NICE adherence audit with clinician worklists.
  • Test letter extraction on a small, consented sample.
Days 61–90

Test, learn and share

  • Run PDSA cycles and measure the effect.
  • Board and commissioner briefing.
  • Conference abstract and service evaluation write-up.
  • Plan for scale-up and the Bengaluru clinic.

What success looks like at 90 days

1 agreed outcomes dataset across adult and Under 18s services
2 improvement projects with SPC evidence of change (or clear learning)
1 safety worklist live and used by the titration team
1 publishable service evaluation and conference abstract
