Routine screening

  • Sleep: Children's Sleep Habits Questionnaire (CSHQ) at baseline and each review, plus sleep diaries (NG87 1.8.17).
  • Eating: an ARFID screen such as the PARDI-AR-Q (parent version 4+, self-report 14+) or the Nine Item ARFID Screen (NIAS).
  • Results feed the titration plan and the safety worklist.

Early-warning flags

  • Positive ARFID screen plus stimulant: dietetic advice before titration, closer weight checks.
  • Weight falling across centile lines (NG87 1.8.5–1.8.6).
  • Sleep onset delayed by more than an hour after a dose change: review dose timing.
  • Melatonin without a sleep plan (CG170 1.7.6–1.7.7): see the NICE audit.

New services and research

  • A neurodevelopmental sleep clinic for children and young people.
  • A feeding and dietetics pathway with ARFID-informed dietitians and psychology.
  • Publishable studies: ARFID prevalence in a Right to Choose population; stimulants, appetite and ARFID; dose timing and sleep.
