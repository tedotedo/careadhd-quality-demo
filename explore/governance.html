Governance

Safe, lawful and trusted by design: UK GDPR, Caldicott, DPIA, clinical safety and a human in the loop.

§UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018

Lawful basis and special category condition agreed with the Data Protection Officer. Clear privacy information for patients and families. Respect the National Data Opt-out where it applies.

8Caldicott principles

Justify every use, use identifiable data only when essential, the minimum necessary, need-to-know access, and the duty to share well for care. Caldicott Guardian sign-off.

DDPIA before anything is built

A Data Protection Impact Assessment for each new use, reviewed when the use changes. DSPT compliance maintained.

PMinimise and pseudonymise

Analytics run on pseudonymised extracts. Small numbers suppressed in reports. Engineering and testing done on synthetic data like this demo, so real data never needs to leave its secure home.

CSClinical safety: DCB0129 / DCB0160

Clinical Safety Officer, hazard log and safety case for any tool that touches care. MHRA guidance on software and AI as a medical device checked before any model influences individual decisions.

HHuman in the loop

Models suggest, clinicians decide. Every alert and AI extraction is reviewable, explainable and auditable, with regular checks for bias and drift.

Service evaluation, audit or research?

Most of this is service evaluation and audit, which can be done under existing governance. Anything intended to generate generalisable knowledge is checked with the HRA decision tool and given ethics approval where needed. Cross-border work with the Bengaluru team uses an agreed international transfer mechanism and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, with synthetic or pseudonymised data by default.
