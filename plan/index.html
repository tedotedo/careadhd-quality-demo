04 · The whole journey · Principle 1

Who acts, and what they see.

Every flag has a named owner and an escalation route.

OvernightThe audit runs

On pseudonymised data. The worklist is ready by the UK morning.

UK morningTreating clinician

Sees their own patients' flags with the standard, the evidence and the gap. Signs off each one: action taken, or not applicable with a reason.

Next nightThe audit checks again

Confirms whether the gap has closed. Unactioned flags escalate to the pathway clinical lead after an agreed interval.

Ready listGP

Receives a shared-care request only for children on the ready list. Returned results are checked nightly.

OversightLead, committee, board

Pathway lead: compliance by standard and team. Governance committee: a monthly report. Board and commissioners: a short assurance summary.

06 · National data returns

One data model, three uses.

Most of the fields the audit reads are ones NHS England already requires from us. If we build it once, it serves the worklist, the commissioner report and the national return.

NHS England Digital · Neurodevelopmental data hubRecording autism and ADHD assessments in the Mental Health Services Data Set (MHSDS)Technical guidance ↗
  1. MHSDS

    NHS-funded ADHD and autism work in a mental-health-commissioned service goes to the Mental Health Services Data Set. This is a Standard Contract requirement (SC28, Schedule 6A), and we already submit monthly. The guidance sets the coding: referral reason, suspected ADHD, ADHD or autism diagnostic assessment, diagnosis with the date given, medication review, and discharge. Where under-18s work is commissioned as community paediatrics, the Community Services Data Set (CSDS) applies instead.

  2. Quick win

    Referrals, contacts and primary diagnoses already flow to MHSDS. Outcome scores, care plans and medication records are the next step. Without a baseline outcome, a child's national waiting-time clock does not stop, so recording these would make our waiting figures and our quality evidence more accurate.

  3. Why now

    The 2026/27 NHS Payment Scheme guidance tells ICBs to pay only when data standards are met. The review found only about 10 of some 120 ADHD providers submitting, and we are one of them.

08 · Risks

What could go wrong, and what we do about it.

Start narrow. Measure in shadow mode first. Review weekly.

  • Data qualityMonitoring may sit in free-text letters, so the audit undercounts.Shadow mode measures this before go-live.
  • Alert fatigueToo many flags and clinicians stop reading them.Start narrow, track accuracy weekly, a named owner for every flag.
  • GP capacitySome practices will decline shared care.The readiness list shows where. It does not solve it.
  • Cross-border dataIndia has no UK adequacy decision.Transfers are settled before identifiable data moves.
  • Key-person dependencyI am part-time.The product owner and champions carry the day-to-day work.
